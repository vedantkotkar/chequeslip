Frequently Asked Questions about Cheque Deposit Slips in India

Can I print a computer-generated cheque deposit slip instead of using the bank booklet?

Yes, absolutely. Indian commercial banks—including HDFC Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank—readily accept computer-printed pay-in slips and annexures.

Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) CTS-2010 cheque clearing regulations, the deposit slip is an administrative receipt. As long as your 16-digit account number, branch, drawer/party name, cheque numbers, and depositor signature are clearly printed and legible, bank cashiers accept them without hesitation.

How do I deposit multiple cheques (5, 10, or 20) at once into my bank account?

When depositing 3 or more cheques, handwriting individual bank slips takes excessive time and clutters your accounting records. Banks officially prefer a Bulk Cheque Deposit Annexure (Schedule).

With ChequeSlip, switch to "Bulk Annexure" mode or click "Paste from Excel". ChequeSlip produces a single, professional A4 schedule containing all cheque numbers, dates, drawee banks, and amounts, with an authorized receiving stamp box for the bank teller.

Is ChequeSlip free and is my sensitive banking information safe?

100% Free & 100% Local-First: ChequeSlip is an open, privacy-focused utility built by Kotkar Labs. There are no fees, subscriptions, or login requirements.

Your account numbers, client names, and cheque amounts are never sent to any server. Everything is stored strictly inside your browser's private LocalStorage sandbox. You can even run ChequeSlip offline without an internet connection.

Can I import cheques directly from Microsoft Excel or Tally?

Yes. Click the "Paste from Excel" button in ChequeSlip. Copy rows from any spreadsheet (Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice) or export from Tally ERP / TallyPrime. ChequeSlip automatically maps your columns (Cheque No, Date, Party Name, Bank, Amount) and converts totals into Indian words (Lakhs and Crores) instantly.

How does recurring party auto-fill save time?

If you receive cheques from the same clients, tenants, or vendors each month, you no longer have to re-type their drawee bank and branch. ChequeSlip features a built-in Frequent Parties Directory. When you start typing a client's name, ChequeSlip auto-completes it and instantly fills their Drawee Bank & Branch.

How does the Google Sheets integration work?

ChequeSlip connects directly to your own Google Drive using a free, self-hosted Google Apps Script webhook (takes under 2 minutes to set up). Each time you deposit a batch, clicking "Sync to Google Sheet" appends the date, cheque numbers, drawee banks, and amounts into your private spreadsheet ledger without third-party tools.

          Banking Rules & Counter Acceptance
        

        
      

      

        
        

          1. Is a computer-printed slip accepted by Indian banks?
        

        

          Yes. Bank branches routinely accept clear A4 computer-printed slips. The Cheque Truncation System (CTS) and RBI rules mandate key details: Account Number, Account Name, Date, Cheque Details, Amount in Words & Figures, and Signatures. ChequeSlip provides both the Bank Copy and Customer Counterfoil.
        


        

          2. How does the Bulk Cheque Annexure / Schedule work?
        

        

          If you are depositing 3, 5, 10, or more cheques at once into the same account, you do not need to fill separate slips for each cheque. Print the Bulk Annexure Schedule and attach it to one standard bank slip (writing "As per Annexure attached: [X] Cheques, Total: ₹[Grand Total]"). All major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak) officially accept this format.

  • Ensure the cheque amount in numbers matches the words on each individual cheque.
    •
